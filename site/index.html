Switch computers. Keep everything.

Ferry backs up your files, turns the same USB drive into an Ubuntu installer, and puts it all back afterwards — your files, Wi-Fi, apps, bookmarks, even your wallpaper. No technical skills needed, and it's free.

Three steps, one USB drive

Your files ride across on the same USB drive that installs Ubuntu. Nothing on your computer is touched until your backup has been checked.

  1. Back up

    Ferry finds your own files — not Windows' — encrypts them with your password, copies them to the USB and checks every one.

  2. Install Ubuntu

    The same USB now starts the Ubuntu installer. Restart from it and install, as the installer guides you.

  3. Restore

    Open the USB in Ubuntu and double-click Restore with Ferry. Enter your password; your computer comes back.

Made for people, not IT departments

Two big buttons and plain words. Ferry explains each step and checks its own work before anything is erased.

  • Nothing to configure

    Plug in a USB drive, pick Ubuntu, set a password. Ferry works out the rest.

  • Checks before it trusts

    Every file is compared with its copy on the USB before Ferry calls the backup done.

  • Honest when it can't

    No Linux version of an app? Ferry says so — or offers a clearly labelled alternative. It never guesses.
